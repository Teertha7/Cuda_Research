B. Validation and sampling
The executed C++ suite passed 984 equivalence checks
plus three analytical checks. It includes dimensions as small
as 1 × 1, non-multiples of CUDA block dimensions, six
CPU execution settings, three GPU block shapes and ﬁve
partitions. Sobel is checked against an independent nested-
mask implementation. Canny’s serial/OpenMP comparison
checks consistency, supplemented by small-input oracle tests
in the artifact; it is not a proof of all possible connectivity
behavior. Every warm-up and retained benchmark output is
also compared with its sequential reference.
Each of 252 conﬁgurations runs in a separate process,
performs three untimed warm-ups, and retains 15 consecutive
observations. Conﬁguration order is shufﬂed once with seed
3129. OpenMP thread counts are 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16; the
sequential path has an inactive parallel region. Dynamic teams
are disabled and passive waiting is requested through the
environment. Afﬁnity and clock frequencies are not ﬁxed. En-
vironment settings are not assumed to override every Microsoft
runtime behavior.
CUDA events delimit H2D, kernel and D2H intervals.
steady_clock measures the complete processing interval.
The raw CSV retains timings, conﬁguration, image family,
repetition, correctness, run identiﬁer and warm-up count.
A manifest records executable/source hashes and hardware
metadata. No samples were removed, and no benchmark
conﬁguration failed.
We report medians and interquartile ranges (IQRs). Pooled
conﬁguration plots contain 45 observations, 15 from each
family; their spread combines content effects and temporal
variation. Speedup plots ﬁrst divide per-family sequential
medians by corresponding implementation medians and then
summarize the three ratios. These need not equal ratios of
pooled medians. There are no conﬁdence intervals or signif-
icance assertions: consecutive samples in one session are not
independent replicated experiments.
C. Held-out model
For hybrid-path conﬁguration medians, we ﬁt
bT = a + bNMf + cNM(1 −f) + dNM min(f, 1 −f), (3)
where NM = N/106. Least squares uses only widths 512,
1024 and 2048 (45 family–fraction medians). All 15 medi-
ans at width 4096 are withheld. Predictions are clipped to
nonnegative values. This four-coefﬁcient descriptive model ap-
proximates a piecewise-linear allocation curve; its coefﬁcients
do not separately identify transfer or synchronization costs.
MAPE is computed on the held-out conﬁguration medians,
not on training observations.
