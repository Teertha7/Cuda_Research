C. Partitions and prediction
Within the hybrid wrapper, pooled best tested CPU fractions
are 1.0, 0.75, 1.0 and 0.5 as width increases. At width 4096,
the half-CPU split has a 6.528 ms median, versus 9.119 ms at
the GPU endpoint and 6.819 ms at the CPU endpoint. How-
ever, independently measured eight-thread OpenMP is 5.996
ms. The small within-wrapper gain over its CPU endpoint
therefore does not establish superiority over a well-performing
CPU-only execution (Fig. 4). Endpoint differences across
separate processes expose sensitivity to execution context.
